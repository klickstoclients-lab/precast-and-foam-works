Frequently Asked Architectural Foam & Precast Questions

Answers to common questions about durability, hurricane protection, weight savings, and custom designs for Florida homes.

How does polyurea-coated EPS architectural foam hold up against Florida hurricanes and coastal salt air?

expand_more
Our architectural EPS profiles are engineered specifically for High-Velocity Hurricane Zones (HVHZ) and coastal environments. Each high-density Expanded Polystyrene core is encapsulated in fiber-reinforced fiberglass mesh, polyurea hard-shell armor, and elastomeric 100% acrylic stone finishes. The result is an impact-resistant, waterproof, and salt-impervious building element that will never rot, delaminate, or attract termites.

What is the weight difference between EPS composite moldings and solid precast concrete or quarried limestone?

expand_more
Polyurea-armored EPS composite elements are up to 80% lighter than traditional solid precast concrete or quarried stone while offering an identical tactile limestone appearance. This dramatic weight reduction eliminates the need for heavy steel lintels, structural framing upgrades, or deep foundation footers—accelerating installation times and lowering overall construction costs.

Can Precast and Foam Works replicate custom historic profiles or fabricate directly from AutoCAD (.DWG) blueprints?

expand_more
Yes. Our New Port Richey facility utilizes 4-axis CNC hot-wire profiling systems that read native AutoCAD (`.DWG`/`.DXF`), Revit BIM files, and physical profile templates. Whether you are matching damaged historic stucco trim on an existing home or creating bespoke monumental cornices, arches, and columns from an architect's drawing, we achieve millimeter-level repeatability.

Are your architectural fireplace mantels, kitchen range hoods, and exterior trims fire-rated?

expand_more
Yes. We utilize ASTM E84 Class A fire-retardant EPS cores paired with cementitious and non-combustible artisan cast limestone shells. For interior hearth surrounds, fireplace mantels, and custom kitchen range hoods, our cast limestone and cement-composite systems meet strict residential and commercial building fire codes.
